ABOUT

Helping freelancers understand the numbers behind their work.

KD-ToolsClub is being built around a simple idea: freelancers should be able to understand what their work is really worth before making important client decisions.

The problem

A project can look attractive because the client offers a large headline price. But the real economics can be very different once meetings, communication, revisions, administration, software, contractors and other costs are included.

KD-ToolsClub focuses on making those hidden factors easier to calculate.

What we're building

The platform will provide free calculators covering project profitability, pricing, effective hourly rates, scope creep, revisions, retainers and client comparisons.

We are also creating small downloadable kits containing practical spreadsheets, templates and checklists.

Our approach

Keep it simple. Show the assumptions. Make the calculations understandable. Avoid unnecessary complexity.

KD-ToolsClub is designed as a practical decision-support resource, not a replacement for professional financial, legal or tax advice.
