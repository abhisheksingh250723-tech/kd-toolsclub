About KD-ToolsClub

Useful tools for people doing business on their own.

KD-ToolsClub is designed around practical decisions: what to charge, what a project is worth, where scope is slipping and how business costs affect the money you keep.

Simple by design

The goal is not to create another complicated dashboard. Each tool should answer one useful question clearly and give you a practical next step.

Built in layers

Free tools help with individual decisions. Digital kits group related resources. Curated stacks bring several tools together around a workflow.
